FAQ

Common questions

How often is the data refreshed?

All data sources are scraped daily. HTTP header signals are re-checked every 24 hours. Change events are logged on every scrape so you can see exactly when a company added or dropped a technology.

How many companies are in the database?

We currently have 300+ companies indexed across tech, fintech, devtools, and SaaS — and we're growing daily. Enterprise customers can request specific companies be added to the scrape list within 24 hours.

What's the confidence score?

Every technology signal has a confidence score from 0 to 1. HTTP headers score 0.92–0.95 (direct evidence from the running app). Job listing mentions score 0.70. When multiple sources agree, the score is boosted up to 0.99.

How do I get my API key?

Instantly — after signing up (free) or completing checkout (paid plans), your API key is displayed on screen. Paid keys are activated immediately after payment via Stripe. Use it in the Authorization: Bearer your_key header.

Is this different from BuiltWith or Wappalyzer?

Yes. BuiltWith focuses on front-end tech from website fingerprinting. We index back-end infrastructure, data tools, and cloud providers inferred from engineering job descriptions — signals that BuiltWith can't detect. And we're 10x cheaper.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel through the Stripe billing portal — no cancellation fees, no questions asked. Your key stays active until the end of your billing period.
